About Me
Built for complexity.
Driven by results.

My supply chain story didn't start in a classroom—it started during a pandemic, with a startup idea and a problem nobody was solving.

In 2020, when COVID kept people home and Diwali was still around the corner, I co-founded Bijli Delivery—a last-mile delivery startup bringing firecrackers directly to customers' doors. People were scared to go out. Demand was real. And nobody was connecting the two. I saw the gap and moved fast.

Building Bijli from scratch meant doing everything myself—negotiating with warehouse vendors, mapping distribution routes, managing inventory for a perishable, hazardous product, and coordinating last-mile logistics under time pressure with zero margin for error. That hands-on chaos is where my passion for supply chain was born. I didn't just learn the theory— I lived the cost of getting it wrong.

That experience led me to formalize the craft through an MS in Supply Chain Management at UT Dallas, then into real-world roles at MTE Industries and PepsiCo—where I learned that the biggest supply chain wins aren't in the warehouse. They're in the data.

Today at The Parts House in Jacksonville, I'm orchestrating inventory transformation across a multi-site distribution network—building the data infrastructure, predictive models, and executive dashboards that turn raw supply chain complexity into competitive advantage.

I don't just analyze supply chains. I redesign them.
